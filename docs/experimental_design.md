# Experimental Design Framework

This document defines the planned AeroFlex V2 experimental framework. Values that have not yet been experimentally determined are marked as TBD.

## Independent Variables

- Wingtip geometry: TBD set of interchangeable three-dimensional wingtip designs
- Angle of attack: TBD
- Airflow velocity: TBD experimental condition after airflow characterization
- Geometry parameters: TBD, including any measurable wingtip dimensions selected for repeatable fabrication

## Derived Operating Parameters

- Reynolds number: calculated from measured airflow velocity, wing chord, and air properties; not directly controlled
- Wing chord: TBD after airflow mapping and final wing-dimension selection
- Air properties used in calculations: TBD from recorded ambient conditions or justified reference values

## Dependent Variables

- Lift force: experimentally measured, units TBD
- Drag force: experimentally measured, units TBD
- Aerodynamic efficiency, L/D: calculated from measured lift and drag
- Measurement uncertainty: TBD from calibration and repeated measurements

## Controlled Variables

- Baseline wing planform and airfoil: TBD
- Wing mounting position and alignment: TBD
- Test distance from airflow source: TBD
- Fan speed setting: TBD during airflow characterization
- Sensor mounting and orientation: TBD
- Ambient conditions recorded during testing: TBD
- Fabrication method and material consistency: TBD

## Repeated Measurements

- Each wingtip geometry will be tested over repeated trials.
- Each selected angle of attack and airflow condition will require repeated force measurements.
- The number of repetitions is TBD until preliminary measurement variability is known.
- Trial order and reset procedure are TBD.

## Uncertainty and Calibration Requirements

- Lift and drag sensors must be zeroed and calibrated before force measurements are treated as results.
- Sensor cross-axis sensitivity and cross-talk must be checked before comparing wingtip geometries.
- Tare or baseline force measurements must be recorded so aerodynamic forces from the wing can be separated from forces acting on the mounting system.
- Sensor range, resolution, and uncertainty are TBD.
- Airflow velocity measurement uncertainty is TBD.
- Alignment uncertainty for angle of attack and force axes is TBD.
- Data processing must preserve raw measurements and document any corrections.

## Planned Lift and Drag Measurement

AeroFlex V2 will measure lift and drag directly using a controlled force-measurement setup. The final sensor arrangement, mounting geometry, and data recording procedure are TBD. No aerodynamic coefficients or force results have been determined yet.

## Airflow Characterization

Airflow characterization will be completed before final V2 wing dimensions are selected. Measurements must be taken at multiple spatial locations across the intended test area to quantify flow non-uniformity. The final test-section location and wing dimensions will be selected only after airflow mapping. Velocity distribution, usable test region, turbulence observations, and repeatability are TBD pending anemometer measurements.
