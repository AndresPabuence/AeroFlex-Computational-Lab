# AeroFlex Project Instructions

## Current project

AeroFlex V2 is an experimental aerospace engineering project studying how wingtip geometry affects aerodynamic efficiency at low Reynolds numbers.

## Important

- The previous free-flight glider experiment is V1.
- V1 was rejected as the primary experimental method because launch conditions, stability, center of gravity, and construction differences were difficult to control.
- Do NOT delete V1.
- AeroFlex V2 is now the main project.

## Scientific rules

- Do not assume that curved, simple, or any other wingtip is superior.
- Experimental evidence must determine the result.
- Do not invent aerodynamic coefficients or experimental data.
- Do not add AI or machine learning merely to make the project appear more advanced.
- Prefer physically justified models and measurable quantities.
- V2 will measure lift and drag of interchangeable wingtip geometries.
- Computational modeling supports the physical experiment; it does not replace it.

## Repository rules

- Preserve project history.
- Do not modify or move files unless explicitly instructed.
- Keep code understandable and scientifically justified.
- Do not generate results that have not actually been measured or simulated.
